# Data licence

The derived data in this repository (the registry, the gold stance timelines, the subset and
bundle indexes, the extracted stance records, scores, answers and figures) is licensed under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0):
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>. The subset index is derived from
canopy-collapse-news-2024-2025 v1.1.0 (CC BY 4.0, doi:10.5281/zenodo.23261738).

The articles belong to their publishers. They are not distributed here: the repository links to
them by URL and identifies their text by hash. Model outputs (paraphrases, summaries, answers)
contain errors; measured error rates are given in docs/RESULTS.md.

Takedown or correction requests: open an issue on the repository.
